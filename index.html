Events in plain English

Patch-note phrases, decoded

"after maintenance"
The event starts when the servers come back up on that date, not at midnight.
"until 11:59 PM UTC"
The last minute you can play or claim. Use the time toggle above to see it in your own clock.
"can be used until … 2:00 AM UTC"
Items you already claimed vanish after this. It's usually 2 hours after the claim deadline.
"near your level"
Monsters within 20 levels above or below you.
"1-person party" / "solo"
Kill the boss on your own. Practice Mode often counts; the patch notes say when it doesn't.
"shared across the account"
You only need to do it on one character.
"Tradable within world"
You can move it between your characters on the same server through storage.
"Karma …"
The item can be traded once more before it locks.
Heroic vs Interactive
Heroic worlds (formerly Reboot) and Interactive (trading) worlds often get different rewards in the notes.

Where to play

Main world
Your regular server (Heroic or Interactive, set with the Server switch). Operation Ultima, Legion, Sunny Sundays and most of the patch happen here.
Challenger World
New season characters only (up to 5). Climb tiers for rewards; Emerald and up can be claimed on your main world. Pick the same type as your main world. Characters can leap out from Dec 16.
Quanah
Only for a Challenger World character marked “Quanah World Leap” at creation, after Lv.260 and 6th job. Roguelite training world until Mar 9. No Operation Ultima or Cash Shop there.
