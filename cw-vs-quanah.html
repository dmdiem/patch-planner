Challenger World
Main goal

Lv.20 Sol Janus enhancer + a usable character

Climb 9 tiers with a new character. Tiers from Emerald up are claimed on your main world.

What reaches your main

  • Emerald (30k): 20 Straight to Lv.200 potions, Legendary potential scroll, 30 Bright Cubes
  • Master (70k + solo Normal Mayrin): 3 armor 18★ scrolls, Straight to Lv.250 potion
  • Challenger (90k + solo Hard Mayrin): Sol Janus Skill Enhancer (Lv.20)
  • The character itself, World Leaped to your main world’s type

  4. Hard Mayrin solo at Lv.280+ hardest step
  5. Claim tiers on your main, then World Leap the character

Quanah
Main goal

The Inspector’s Mapae badge

A limited-time roguelite world. Only a Challenger World character marked “Quanah World Leap” at creation can go.

What reaches your main

  • Inspector’s Mapae: badge with attack from Star Force, up to 22★. Moves once to another character on your account
  • The character itself (Lv.260+), leaping to the same world type in March
  • Gear like Eternal pieces, up to 22★. Libae’s gear stays behind

What you play there

  • +1,000% EXP buff, Proving Fields, Battlesim dungeon (150 floors a week)
  • Inspector Level resets to 1 when you die; modules and inherited skills carry over
  • No cubes, flames, Cash Shop, rental gear, Operation Ultima or MVP perks

Effort

  1. Tick Quanah World Leap when creating a Challenger-Heroic character. Can’t be added later
  2. Level to 260 with Hyper Burning MAX and finish 6th job
  3. Prep: Hero’s Echo-type skills, gear, Android, empty Use tab
  4. Farm gear, Quanah Crystals and boss passes in Quanah
  5. Black Mage (Hard or Extreme) for the badge hardest step
  6. Star Force + potential the badge for your main’s stat before leaving
Done by Mar 9 everyone is moved out

The catch if you want both

Two characters

A Quanah character can’t join Challenger World events, so tiers need a separate character.

One Hyper Burning payout

Hyper Burning MAX level rewards are once per account. Only one of the two gets them.

Your main’s routine still runs

Operation Ultima, Shadow Bounties and Sunny Sundays go on alongside either one. A Quanah character can’t join Operation Ultima at all.
